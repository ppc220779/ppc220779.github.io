Privacy Policy

Home Server Backup is a personal application used only by its owner. It uses rclone to access the owner’s Google Drive to upload, retrieve and manage encrypted home-server backup archives.

The application requests Google Drive access to perform these operations. Access credentials are held on the owner’s server; they are not published on this website. Backup archives are encrypted before upload. The app does not sell or share Google user data with third parties. Google Drive stores the encrypted backups subject to Google’s applicable terms. The owner can revoke access using Google Account permissions at any time.

This page describes this personal application and may be updated if its use changes.
